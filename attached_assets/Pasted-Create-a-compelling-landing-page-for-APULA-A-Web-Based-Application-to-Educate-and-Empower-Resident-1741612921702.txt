APULA: A Web-Based Application to Educate and Empower Residents in Fire Prevention Through Engaging Games

'Learn fire safety in an interactive way! Engage in fun and educational games designed to equip you with life-saving knowledge.'

Quiz Game: Test your fire safety knowledge.
