About APULA

APULA is an innovative web-based platform designed to educate communities on fire prevention through interactive and engaging games. Our goal is to make learning about fire safety fun, accessible, and memorable for everyone.

Fire-related accidents can be prevented with proper knowledge and awareness. APULA bridges the gap between traditional fire safety education and modern interactive learning, making critical safety information more engaging and memorable.

Our Educational Games

4 Pics 1 Word

Identify fire safety-related terms by looking at four images that have something in common.

Crossword

Test your knowledge of fire safety terms and concepts by solving challenging crossword puzzles.

Guess the Missing Letter

Learn fire-related terminology by filling in missing letters in words related to fire safety.

PPE Game

Learn about personal protective equipment by matching gear with its purpose in fire safety.

Quiz Game

Test your fire safety knowledge with challenging multiple-choice questions.

Word Scrambler

Unscramble important fire prevention words to reinforce your vocabulary.

Common Causes of Fire

  • Unattended cooking equipment
  • Electrical systems and appliances
  • Heating equipment
  • Smoking materials
  • Candles and open flames

Education plays a crucial role in reducing fire hazards. By understanding the causes of fires and proper preventive measures, individuals can significantly reduce the risk of fire-related incidents in their homes and communities.
